N8n Workflow Automation

Here's the part most people get wrong. n8n is a workflow automation tool in the spirit of Zapier that you can self-host for free.

Which, in day-to-day terms, means: It is fair-code (not strictly open source, which the project is upfront about) and runs entirely on your own machine with a Docker container. The free personal use is unlimited; teams pay only when they outgrow the hobby tier. For a blog it handles the glue: fetch, transform, publish, notify.
