<section class="fact-grid" aria-label="Key points">
  <article class="fact-card fact-card-highlight">
    <p class="card-label">VERIFIED HUMAN RECORD</p>
    <p class="fact-value">122 <span>years</span></p>
    <p class="fact-detail">Jeanne Calment lived 122 years and 164 days. A record is not proof of a biological ceiling.</p>
    <a href="https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/oldest-person">Guinness record <span aria-hidden="true">↗</span></a>
  </article>
  <article class="fact-card">
    <p class="card-label">WHAT THE TOY MODELS SAY</p>
    <p class="fact-value fact-text">No maximum</p>
    <p class="fact-detail">Our simulations do not model age-specific human mortality and cannot predict a maximum lifespan.</p>
    <a href="{{ '/biological-immortality/' | relative_url }}">See assumptions and limits <span aria-hidden="true">→</span></a>
  </article>
  <article class="fact-card">
    <p class="card-label">WHAT DEMOGRAPHY PROJECTS</p>
    <p class="fact-value fact-text">126–130?</p>
    <p class="fact-detail">One model forecasts possible record ages this century; its probabilities are conditional forecasts, not a biological bound.</p>
    <a href="https://doi.org/10.4054/demres.2021.44.52">Read Pearce &amp; Raftery (2021) <span aria-hidden="true">↗</span></a>
  </article>
</section>

<section class="content-section">
  <div class="section-heading">
    <p class="eyebrow">THE RESEARCH FRAME</p>
    <h2>Three bottlenecks, one systems question</h2>
  </div>
  <div class="topic-grid">
    <article class="topic-card">
      <span class="topic-number">01</span>
      <h3>Renewal without cancer escape</h3>
      <p>Can tissue renewal be supported while avoiding a universal, unchecked growth signal?</p>
    </article>
    <article class="topic-card">
      <span class="topic-number">02</span>
      <h3>Mutation burden without blind editing</h3>
      <p>Can risky lineages be detected and replaced without treating every neutral mutation as damage?</p>
    </article>
    <article class="topic-card">
      <span class="topic-number">03</span>
      <h3>Neural repair without losing the person</h3>
      <p>Can a memory-bearing brain be maintained in place while preserving its functional state?</p>
    </article>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="callout">
  <div>
    <p class="eyebrow">A NECESSARY DISTINCTION</p>
    <h2>Longer life is not the same as immortality.</h2>
    <p>The report separates an observed longevity record, demographic forecasts, unvalidated physiological extrapolations, and our own deliberately hypothetical simulations. None establishes that humans can live indefinitely.</p>
